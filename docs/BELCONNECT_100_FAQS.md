## Chapter 1: BelConnect Basics & General Information

### Q1: What is BelConnect?
**Answer:**  
BelConnect is Belagavi’s dedicated hyperlocal on-demand service and job marketplace. It connects local residents with verified service professionals (such as electricians, plumbers, carpenters, cleaning staff, and AC technicians) and connects local job seekers with nearby employers across the Belagavi district.

### Q2: Is BelConnect available only in Belagavi?
**Answer:**  
Yes. BelConnect is built specifically for Belagavi (Belgaum), Karnataka. By focusing exclusively on our local city and surrounding talukas, we ensure faster response times, verified neighborhood technicians, and direct community trust.

### Q3: Which specific localities in Belagavi are covered?
**Answer:**  
BelConnect covers all major neighborhoods in Belagavi, including:
- **Central & West:** Tilakwadi, Camp, Shahapur, Hindwadi, Vadgaon, Bhagya Nagar, Angol, RPD Cross, Khanapur Road.
- **North & East:** Channamma Nagar, Auto Nagar, Kanbargi, Nehrunagar, Club Road, Subhash Nagar.
- **Industrial & Tech Zones:** Udyambag, Machhe, Kakati Industrial Area.  
You can also choose "Belagavi (All Areas)" in the search bar to find city-wide coverage.

### Q4: What languages can I use on BelConnect?
**Answer:**  
BelConnect supports 4 languages:
1. **English**
2. **Kannada (ಕನ್ನಡ)**
3. **Marathi (मराठी)**
4. **Hindi (हिन्दी)**  
The entire interface, navigation menus, booking confirmations, and guided tours adapt to your preferred language.

### Q5: How do I switch the language to Kannada, Marathi, or Hindi?
**Answer:**  
- **On Desktop / Laptop:** Click the language dropdown located in the top-right header menu next to your profile.
- **On Android Phone / Mobile Browser:** Tap the menu icon (three horizontal lines) in the top-right corner, scroll down to the **Language** section, and tap your preferred language card (English, ಕನ್ನಡ, मराठी, or हिन्दी).

### Q6: Is BelConnect completely free to download and use?
**Answer:**  
Yes! Downloading the app, browsing service categories, searching for professionals, and exploring local job openings is 100% free. You only pay for the specific service work you contract with the technician.

### Q7: Can I use BelConnect on an iPhone or computer, or is it Android-only?
**Answer:**  
BelConnect is accessible on all devices:
- **Android:** Via the official downloadable APK package or web browser.
- **iPhone / iPad / Mac / Windows PC:** Open any modern browser (Safari, Chrome, Firefox, Edge) and visit `https://belcconect.vercel.app`. You can also "Add to Home Screen" on iOS Safari to use it just like a native app.

### Q8: What is the difference between opening the website and installing the Android APK?
**Answer:**  
Both provide the same full features, but the Android APK offers:
- Instant launch from your phone home screen without browser toolbars.
- Reliable background notifications for job requests and technician arrivals.
- Smooth background GPS broadcast for service providers when travelling.

### Q9: Do I need an active internet connection to use BelConnect?
**Answer:**  
Yes. BelConnect requires mobile data (4G/5G) or Wi-Fi to load real-time provider availability, live map GPS positions, in-app voice calls, and direct messaging.

### Q10: Is BelConnect an agency that employs technicians directly?
**Answer:**  
No. BelConnect is a trusted hyperlocal marketplace platform. We empower independent, self-employed local technicians, small business owners, and local shops in Belagavi by connecting them directly with household customers with zero middleman commissions.

### Q11: Who are the service professionals listed on BelConnect?
**Answer:**  
They are verified local technicians and trade specialists residing and working within Belagavi. Each professional undergoes Government ID verification (Aadhaar/PAN) and selfie matching before receiving a **Verified** trust badge.

### Q12: What types of services can I book on BelConnect?
**Answer:**  
You can book services across 10+ core categories:
- **Electrical:** Wiring, fan installation, switchboard repair, MCB fixes.
- **Plumbing:** Pipe leaks, tap replacement, water tank cleaning, bathroom fittings.
- **AC & Appliance Repair:** AC servicing, refrigerator repair, washing machine fixes, water purifier (RO) filter changes.
- **Cleaning & Pest Control:** Deep home cleaning, kitchen cleaning, sofa shampooing, cockroach & termite treatments.
- **Carpentry & Painting:** Furniture repairs, door lock fitting, interior/exterior painting.
- **Salon & Grooming:** Men's haircut, women's salon services at home.
- **Home Tutors & Helpers:** Daily assistance, local tutoring, and handyman tasks.

## Chapter 2: Customer Account, Registration & Profile Security

### Q13: How do I create a customer account on BelConnect?
**Answer:**  
1. Open the BelConnect app or website.
2. Tap the **Log In / Register** button in the top navigation.
3. Choose **Customer Registration**.
4. Enter your full name, 10-digit Indian mobile number, and password, or simply tap **Continue with Google**.
5. Once submitted, your customer account is active instantly.

### Q14: Can I sign in using my Google account?
**Answer:**  
Yes. Tap the **Continue with Google** button on the login screen. On the native Android APK, a secure mobile authentication handoff connects your Google profile and creates your session with a single tap.

### Q15: How does Phone OTP login work?
**Answer:**  
Enter your 10-digit mobile number on the login screen and tap **Send OTP**. You will receive an SMS containing a 6-digit one-time code. Enter the code in the verification boxes to sign in securely without needing a password.

### Q16: What should I do if I don’t receive the SMS OTP code?
**Answer:**  
1. Ensure your phone has good mobile network reception.
2. Check that your 10-digit number was entered without the leading `+91` or `0`.
3. Wait for the 60-second cooldown timer to finish, then tap **Resend Code**.
4. If SMS delivery is delayed by your telecom carrier, you can always sign in instantly using your Google account or password.

### Q17: Can one person have both a Customer and a Service Provider profile?
**Answer:**  
Yes. You can use your customer account to book home repairs, and if you offer trade skills, you can register a Service Provider profile with your business details under the same phone number or email.

### Q18: How do I switch between Customer view and Provider dashboard?
**Answer:**  
If your account has provider privileges:
1. Tap your profile avatar in the top-right corner.
2. Select **Provider Dashboard** from the menu.
3. To return to booking services as a customer, click the **Customer Marketplace** or **Home** button in the dashboard navigation bar.

### Q19: How do I update my profile details, name, or phone number?
**Answer:**  
1. Tap your profile icon and select **My Profile** (or visit `/account`).
2. Update your full name, profile picture, or email address.
3. Tap **Save Changes**. (To update your phone number, an OTP confirmation is required to prevent unauthorized changes).

### Q20: How do I save and manage multiple home/office addresses?
**Answer:**  
1. In your **Account** screen, scroll to **Saved Addresses**.
2. Tap **Add New Address**.
3. Label it (e.g., *Home*, *Parents' House*, *Shop in Tilakwadi*).
4. Pin your location on the interactive map and enter nearby landmark details.
5. During any future booking, you can select this saved address with a single tap.

### Q21: What happens if I forget my password?
**Answer:**  
On the login screen, click **Forgot Password?**. Enter your registered email address or phone number. We will send a secure password reset link or SMS code allowing you to set a fresh password immediately.

### Q22: Is my personal information and contact number kept safe?
**Answer:**  
Yes. BelConnect implements strict data safety standards:
- All data travels over encrypted HTTPS (TLS 1.3).
- Your live location is only shared during active bookings and shuts off automatically once the job is completed.
- Phone numbers are protected against scraping, and voice calls use in-app private audio connections.

### Q23: Can I delete my account if I no longer wish to use the service?
**Answer:**  
Yes. You have full control over your data. In your account settings, tap **Account Deletion Request**. For security and fraud prevention, your request is reviewed within 24 hours to ensure there are no ongoing active jobs or unpaid services before all personal records are scrubbed.

### Q24: Why did I receive a "Session Expired" notice, and how do I log back in?
**Answer:**  
BelConnect uses secure server-validated sessions. If you have been inactive for several weeks or changed devices, your session token refreshes for your security. Simply enter your credentials or tap Google Sign-In to resume immediately.

## Chapter 3: Finding Services & Placing a Booking

### Q25: How do I search for a specific technician or repair service?
**Answer:**  
At the top of the Home page:
1. Tap the search input box that reads *"Search for Painter, Electrician, Plumber..."*.
2. Type the service name or problem (e.g., "tap leak", "water heater", "switchboard").
3. Tap **Search** to see a list of matched local experts in Belagavi.

### Q26: How does the area filter in the search bar help me?
**Answer:**  
Next to the search input, there is a location pill (e.g., *"Belagavi (All Areas)"*). Tapping it lets you filter technicians specifically based in your neighborhood—such as *Shahapur*, *Tilakwadi*, *Udyambag*, or *Hindwadi*—so you get someone who can reach your house within 20–30 minutes.

### Q27: How can I browse through the service categories?
**Answer:**  
On the home page, scroll down to the **Browse All Categories** grid. Tap on any category card (e.g., *Electrical*, *Plumbing*, *Cleaning*, *AC Repair*) to view all available service packages and listed technicians in that category.

### Q28: What do the sort filters ("Recommended", "Rating: High to Low", "Most Experienced") mean?
**Answer:**  
When viewing providers in a category:
- **Recommended:** Balances proximity to your area, active online status, and customer satisfaction.
- **Rating (High to Low):** Shows technicians with the highest 5-star customer review scores first.
- **Most Experienced:** Shows veterans with the highest number of completed jobs in Belagavi.

### Q29: What does the "Verified" green badge next to a provider's name mean?
**Answer:**  
The **Verified** badge indicates that the BelConnect Trust & Safety moderation team has reviewed and confirmed the technician’s government photo ID (eKYC) and identity. You can book them with confidence.

### Q30: What does "Mutual Pricing" or "Mutually Agreed" mean on a service card?
**Answer:**  
Many repair jobs (such as complex pipe repairs, house painting, or rewiring) cannot have a single fixed price without inspecting the issue. "Mutual Pricing" means the technician will inspect the work on-site, explain the required parts and labor, and agree upon a fair, transparent price with you before starting work.

### Q31: How do fixed-price and hourly-rate services work?
**Answer:**  
- **Fixed Price (e.g., ₹299):** The standard labor cost for straightforward tasks (e.g., installing a ceiling fan or cleaning an AC filter) is fixed upfront. Replacement spare parts (if required) are extra.
- **Hourly Rate (e.g., ₹150/hr):** You pay according to the time spent on multi-task repairs.

### Q32: How do I pin my exact house location on the map during booking?
**Answer:**  
On the booking checkout screen:
1. Tap the **Locate Me** button to automatically center the map on your current GPS coordinates.
2. Drag the blue pin until it points directly at your building or gate.
3. This ensures the technician’s navigation routes directly to your doorstep without making multiple phone calls for directions.

### Q33: Can I type my address manually if GPS is inaccurate or unavailable?
**Answer:**  
Yes! If GPS is weak inside your building, tap **Enter Address Manually**. You can fill in your House/Flat number, Building name, Street name, and nearby Landmark (e.g., *"Opposite Tilakwadi Post Office"*).

### Q34: Can I select a specific date and time slot for the service?
**Answer:**  
Yes. On the booking page, select:
- **Service Date:** Today, Tomorrow, or pick a custom date from the calendar.
- **Time Slot:** Choose from convenient 2-hour windows (e.g., *09:00 AM – 11:00 AM*, *02:00 PM – 04:00 PM*, or *06:00 PM – 08:00 PM*).

### Q35: How do I add special instructions or landmark notes for the technician?
**Answer:**  
In the booking summary form, there is a text field labeled **Additional Notes / Instructions**. You can write details like: *"Please bring a tall ladder"*, *"Main doorbell is not working, please call upon arrival"*, or *"Near Ganesh Temple"*.

### Q36: What happens immediately after I tap "Confirm Booking"?
**Answer:**  
Your booking is created in **Pending** status. The selected service provider receives an instant push notification and sound alert with your requested service, area, and time slot.

### Q37: How long does a service provider have to accept my booking request?
**Answer:**  
Providers typically accept within 10 to 15 minutes. A live countdown status bar appears on your booking details screen keeping you informed in real time.

### Q38: What happens if my booking request expires without being accepted?
**Answer:**  
If the provider is currently engaged in another repair and cannot respond before the timer expires, the booking automatically switches to **Expired**. You will see a button to instantly choose another nearby technician or re-request with one tap.

### Q39: Can I book more than one service at the same time?
**Answer:**  
Yes. You can book an electrician for a switchboard fix and a plumber for a kitchen drain at the same time. Each booking has its own independent tracking, chat, and status card under **My Bookings**.

### Q40: Why does the app say "No service provider is available in your area right now"?
**Answer:**  
This message appears if all verified professionals in that specific category are currently offline, fully booked, or outside your selected area. You can tap **Choose Another Service**, expand your area filter to "Belagavi (All Areas)", or check back in a few minutes as technicians complete their current jobs.

## Chapter 4: Live GPS Tracking, In-App Calling & Messaging

### Q41: How does live tracking of the service provider work?
**Answer:**  
Once the technician accepts your request and taps **Start Travel**, their real-time GPS position appears on your booking map. You can watch their marker move toward your home with an updated estimated time of arrival (ETA).

### Q42: When does the provider’s live GPS location appear on my map?
**Answer:**  
Live tracking begins as soon as the provider marks the status as **On the Way / Travelling**. Prior to that, while they are preparing their toolkit or finishing a prior appointment, the map shows *"Waiting for provider's live location..."*.

### Q43: What does "Provider location temporarily unavailable" mean?
**Answer:**  
This indicates that the technician’s phone temporarily lost cellular data reception (such as passing through a basement or rural pocket on the outskirts of Belagavi) or that GPS signal is recalibrating. As soon as their phone reconnects, their position updates automatically.

### Q44: Can the service provider see my live location too?
**Answer:**  
No. The provider only sees the destination delivery address pin you confirmed during booking. If you wish to guide them in real time while waiting outside your gate, you can tap **Share Live Location** to grant a temporary, privacy-protected window.

### Q45: How do I make a voice call to my service provider inside the app?
**Answer:**  
On your booking screen, tap the round blue **Call** button with the phone icon. The app initiates an in-app voice call over your internet connection.

### Q46: Does calling through the app reveal my private mobile phone number?
**Answer:**  
No! In-app voice calling routes through an encrypted audio stream (WebRTC) directly between the two devices. Neither your phone number nor the provider’s phone number is dialed over the public cellular network.

### Q47: What should I do if the call doesn't connect or disconnects midway?
**Answer:**  
1. Check that you have granted BelConnect **Microphone** permission in your phone settings.
2. Confirm you have an active internet connection.
3. If internet bandwidth is low, tap the **Chat** button right next to the Call button to send an instant text message instead.

### Q48: Can I send text messages and chat with the provider before they arrive?
**Answer:**  
Yes. Tap the **Chat** button on the booking page. You can send text messages to coordinate exact arrival timing, discuss spare parts, or clarify directions. Messages are delivered instantly in real time.

### Q49: Can I share photos of the broken appliance in the chat?
**Answer:**  
Yes. In the chat window, tap the camera/attachment icon to snap or upload a photo of the damaged switchboard, leaking pipe, or AC unit. This helps the technician bring the right replacement tools and parts.

### Q50: How will I know when the service provider has arrived at my doorstep?
**Answer:**  
When the provider reaches your gate, they tap **Arrived** in their app. You will receive an instant notification on your screen, and their status updates to *"Provider has arrived"*.

### Q51: What should I do if the provider is running late?
**Answer:**  
Check their live location marker on the map. If they are held up in traffic (e.g., at Congress Road or RPD Railway Gate), tap the **Call** or **Chat** button to get a quick update. If they are significantly delayed, you can reschedule or cancel without penalty.

### Q52: Can I call customer support if the provider is unresponsive?
**Answer:**  
Yes. If an accepted provider does not answer your chat or call within 15 minutes of the scheduled time, tap the **Help / Support** button at the bottom of the booking page to connect directly with the BelConnect support team.

## Chapter 5: Payments, Cancellations & Reviews

### Q53: How do I pay for the completed service?
**Answer:**  
Payment is made directly upon successful completion of the job. Once the technician finishes the repair and you have verified the work, you pay the agreed amount directly to the technician.

### Q54: Can I pay using UPI (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm) or cash?
**Answer:**  
Yes! All verified service providers accept direct UPI payments (by showing their PhonePe, GPay, or Paytm QR code) as well as direct cash.

### Q55: Are there any hidden platform fees or booking charges on BelConnect?
**Answer:**  
No. BelConnect does not add surprise convenience fees, booking surcharges, or hidden platform taxes at checkout. You only pay for the service and parts agreed upon.

### Q56: How can I cancel a booking that I previously requested?
**Answer:**  
1. Open **My Bookings** from the bottom bar or user menu.
2. Select the active booking.
3. Tap the red **Cancel Booking** button.
4. Select a reason (e.g., *Booked by mistake*, *Issue resolved*, *Provider delayed*) and confirm.

### Q57: Is there any cancellation penalty or fee if I cancel?
**Answer:**  
No. Cancelling a booking is completely free. We simply request that you cancel promptly if your plans change, so the technician can accept other local customers.

### Q58: What happens if the service provider cancels my booking?
**Answer:**  
If a technician experiences an emergency and has to decline or cancel, your booking updates immediately with an alert. You can re-book another available technician in your neighborhood with a single tap.

### Q59: What should I do if a technician does not show up at the scheduled time?
**Answer:**  
If a provider fails to arrive and does not communicate, open the booking and select **Report No-Show**. The booking is closed, the provider's reliability rating is penalized by our moderation team, and you can rebook a different technician immediately.

### Q60: How do I rate and leave a review for the technician after the job?
**Answer:**  
As soon as the technician marks the job as **Completed**:
1. A **Review & Rating** modal automatically appears on your screen.
2. Select a star rating from 1 to 5 stars.
3. Write a brief comment about their punctuality, workmanship, and behavior.
4. Tap **Submit Review**. Your feedback helps other Belagavi residents find the best experts.

### Q61: Can a service provider delete or hide a negative review from their profile?
**Answer:**  
No. Service providers cannot alter or delete customer reviews. All ratings and feedback are authentic and transparent, fostering genuine community accountability across Belagavi.

### Q62: How do I view my past bookings and receipts?
**Answer:**  
Tap the **Bookings** tab in the bottom navigation bar. You will see two tabs:
- **Active:** Currently ongoing or upcoming appointments.
- **History:** All completed and past bookings with dates, prices, technician names, and review details.

### Q63: What should I do if I am dissatisfied with the quality of the repair work?
**Answer:**  
If the repair is faulty or the issue recurs shortly after service:
1. Contact the technician via chat to request a follow-up inspection.
2. If unresolved, go to `/support` or tap **Need Help?** on the booking page to report the issue. BelConnect’s Trust & Safety team will mediate to arrange a rework or resolution.

### Q64: How do I file a formal complaint or request a rework?
**Answer:**  
Visit `/support` or email `support@belconnect.in` with your Booking ID and description of the issue. Our support team responds within 4 business hours.

## Chapter 6: Service Provider Onboarding, eKYC & Trust Badge

### Q65: Who can register as a Service Provider on BelConnect?
**Answer:**  
Any skilled technician, trade professional, contractor, or local repair shop owner working in the Belagavi area can register (e.g., electricians, plumbers, carpenters, mechanics, beauticians, cleaners, appliance repair specialists).

### Q66: Is there any registration fee or commission taken from service providers?
**Answer:**  
No! BelConnect operates on a fair, partner-first model. There are zero upfront registration fees and zero commission cuts on your everyday service earnings. What you earn from the customer is 100% yours.

### Q67: What documents are required for provider eKYC verification?
**Answer:**  
To complete eKYC, you need:
1. A valid Government Photo ID (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, or Voter ID).
2. Your 10-digit mobile phone number.
3. A clear selfie photo captured directly using your phone camera.

### Q68: How do I upload my Aadhaar card or Government ID?
**Answer:**  
1. In your Provider Dashboard, tap the **eKYC Verification** banner or go to `/provider/ekyc`.
2. Select your document type (Aadhaar, PAN, or Voter ID).
3. Enter the document number.
4. Tap **Upload Photo** to attach a clear, readable photo of your original document.

### Q69: Why does the app ask for a live selfie photo during eKYC?
**Answer:**  
The live selfie confirms that the person uploading the document is the actual owner of the credential. This prevents impersonation, protects your business reputation, and ensures customer safety across Belagavi.

### Q70: How long does it take for my provider account to be verified and approved?
**Answer:**  
Our local Trust & Safety verification team audits submitted documents and selfies within **24 hours** (often within 2 to 4 hours during business days).

### Q71: What should I do while my profile shows "Verification Pending"?
**Answer:**  
While waiting:
- Add your services, descriptions, and custom pricing under **My Services**.
- Upload portfolio photos of past jobs to make your profile attractive.
- As soon as our admin approves your KYC, your services go live automatically.

### Q72: Why did my verification status get rejected, and how do I fix it?
**Answer:**  
Common reasons for rejection include blurry document photos, cut-off document corners, or a selfie that doesn’t match the ID photo. If rejected, your dashboard will display a red alert stating the reason with a **Re-upload KYC** button so you can submit a clear photo.

### Q73: Can I receive customer bookings before my eKYC is approved by the admin?
**Answer:**  
No. To maintain strict community trust and safety, unverified providers do not appear in customer searches or category listings until their credentials are authenticated.

### Q74: Why is having the "Verified Expert" badge important for getting bookings?
**Answer:**  
Customers in Belagavi actively prioritize technicians who display the green **Verified Expert** shield. Verified profiles receive up to 4x more booking clicks and higher conversion rates.

### Q75: Can I edit my business name, phone number, or profile photo after verification?
**Answer:**  
Yes. You can edit your display name, business description, and profile picture anytime in **Provider Profile**. If you need to change your registered legal ID or mobile number, a quick verification check is re-triggered to maintain trust.

### Q76: What happens if I move my business to a different area in Belagavi?
**Answer:**  
Simply go to your **Provider Profile**, update your neighborhood locality (e.g., changing from *Shahapur* to *Tilakwadi*), and save. Your profile will immediately start matching customers in your new area.

## Chapter 7: Provider Daily Operations, Services & Job Execution

### Q77: How do I create a new service offering on my profile?
**Answer:**  
1. Open your **Provider Dashboard**.
2. Tap **Add New Service** (or go to `/provider/services/new`).
3. Enter the service title (e.g., *"Ceiling Fan Installation"*).
4. Choose the appropriate Category (e.g., *Electrical*).
5. Select the Pricing Model (Fixed, Hourly, or Mutually Agreed).
6. Add a brief description and tap **Publish Service**.

### Q78: How do I set pricing for my services (Fixed rate vs Mutual pricing)?
**Answer:**  
- For routine, well-defined jobs, enter a specific rupee amount (e.g., ₹250).
- For variable jobs where repair scope depends on on-site inspection, select **Mutually Agreed / Custom Quote**. Customers will know you inspect and agree on price before starting.

### Q79: Can I upload photos of my previous work or shop in my service gallery?
**Answer:**  
Yes! You can attach photos to each service you offer. Profiles featuring real photos of completed repairs, neat installations, or trade tools get significantly more booking requests.

### Q80: What does the "Available for Jobs (ON/OFF)" toggle button do?
**Answer:**  
- **Available for Jobs (ON - Green):** Signals to the system that you are on-duty and ready to take appointments. Your services appear in customer search results.
- **Unavailable (OFF - Grey):** Hides your profile from new instant bookings when you are off-duty, resting, or busy with personal work.

### Q81: Why is it important to turn my availability OFF when I am busy or off-duty?
**Answer:**  
If a customer sends a booking request while you are unavailable and the request times out or is declined, your acceptance rate decreases. Turning your toggle to **OFF** ensures you never receive unwanted alerts while resting.

### Q82: How do I get notified when a customer sends a booking request?
**Answer:**  
When a customer books you, your phone plays an audible incoming job chime and displays an on-screen alert card showing the customer’s name, service required, neighborhood area, and requested time slot.

### Q83: How do I accept or decline an incoming booking request?
**Answer:**  
On the incoming request card:
- Tap the green **Accept** button if you can take the job.
- Tap **Decline** if you cannot fulfill the request. The customer is immediately notified so they can book another professional without waiting.

### Q84: How do I start travelling to the customer's house and share my live location?
**Answer:**  
1. Open the accepted booking under **My Bookings**.
2. Tap **Start Travel / On My Way**.
3. The app begins broadcasting your GPS position to the customer's map so they know you are coming and can open the gate for you.

### Q85: What should I do if Android asks for background location permission?
**Answer:**  
Tap **Allow While Using App** or **Allow All the Time**. This is required so that while you are riding your two-wheeler with your phone in your pocket, the app can smoothly update your arrival time on the customer's map.

### Q86: How do I navigate to the customer's house using Google Maps?
**Answer:**  
On the booking detail screen, tap the **Open in Google Maps** icon next to the customer's address pin. Google Maps will launch automatically with turn-by-turn voice navigation straight to their gate.

### Q87: How do I mark a job as "In Progress" and "Completed"?
**Answer:**  
- When you arrive at the house and begin work, tap **Start Job / In Progress**.
- When the repair is finished and tested, tap **Complete Job**.
- Enter the final collected amount and tap **Confirm Completion**. The customer is then prompted to rate your service.

### Q88: What should I do if a customer is not at home or not answering calls?
**Answer:**  
1. Tap the in-app **Call** or **Chat** button to notify them that you have arrived.
2. Wait 10 minutes at the location.
3. If they remain unreachable, tap **Report Customer No-Show**. The job will close without any penalty to your acceptance rating.

## Chapter 8: Belagavi Jobs & Hiring Portal

### Q89: What is the BelConnect "Jobs & Hiring" portal?
**Answer:**  
It is an integrated job exchange within BelConnect designed to help local Belagavi businesses, shops, restaurants, clinics, and contractors find workers—and help local job seekers find employment close to home.

### Q96: Why does BelConnect ask for Location, Camera, and Microphone permissions?
